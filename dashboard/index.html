200 pre-token protocols, ranked by what a launch would be worth

The watchlist is the floor: every protocol with at least $50M of TVL and no token on record. The radar adds rankable protocols the watchlist rule left out, above and below that floor. Implied value is this entry’s model, not a price and not a promise that a token ever launches.

How implied value is computed

implied = round( TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chain breadth × version discount )

Growth is 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −60%, +120%) ÷ 100 × 0.6. Maturity is 0.70 + min(age, 3 years) ÷ 3 years × 0.50, where age is the time since DefiLlama first listed the protocol; unknown age counts as 365 days. Chain breadth adds 2.5% per extra chain, up to 8. A versioned name (V2, v3, 2.0) is discounted 15%.

  • 0.40 Dexs, Derivatives, Perpetuals, Options — fees flow to the token
  • 0.38 Lending, CDP, Collateral — fees flow to the token
  • 0.34–0.36 Yield, Yield Aggregator, Leveraged Farming
  • 0.28–0.30 Basis Trading, Prediction, Launchpad, Stablecoins, Payments, Insurance
  • 0.14–0.16 Risk Curators, Capital Allocators, RWA — mostly custodied assets, thin fees
  • 0.25 everything else

A protocol “records no token” when DefiLlama records no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol for it. That is the record, not proof it has none. Protocols whose token already trades are set aside in the Live tab at their market cap, never as an implied value.
